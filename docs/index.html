Customer Support & FAQ

Need help or have a feature request? We're here for you.

How does workout syncing work?

Shoe Garage uses Apple's native HealthKit observer query. When your Apple Watch or iPhone saves an outdoor or indoor running/walking workout, the app is gently woken to notify you and queue the run for quick assignment.

Can I switch between miles and kilometers?

Yes. Shoe Garage includes a full dual-unit preference toggle in Settings that instantly updates all paces, wear rates, and distances across the entire app.
